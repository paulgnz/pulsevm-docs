# Who is behind PulseVM

PulseVM is built by **Metallicus**, the team behind XPR Network and Metal Blockchain. For a bank or credit union, the question is not only whether the technology works but whether the company behind it already operates in regulated finance. Here is the public record.

## The company

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Founded** | 2015, by Marshall Hayner (CEO) and Glenn Mariën, who created PulseVM ([source](https://metallicus.com/blog/cornerstone-credit-union-league-stablecoins)) |
| **Based** | United States |
| **Networks** | Core developer of XPR Network and Metal Blockchain, where PulseVM runs |
| **Security controls** | Announced a SOC 2 compliance audit by an independent third-party auditor in 2023 ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/metallicus_metallicus-attains-soc2-compliance-certification-activity-7019407902905917440-FSOs)) |

## Already on the Federal Reserve's instant payment rail

Metallicus completed the Federal Reserve's certification program and has supported **live transactions on the FedNow Service since October 22, 2024**, for sending, receiving and liquidity management ([source](https://metallicus.com/blog/fednow)). In March 2026 it added **Request for Payment** certification, so credit unions can send biller-presented payment requests over FedNow ([source](https://metallicus.com/blog/metallicus-expands-fednow-service-capabilities-with-request-for-payment-rfp-certification)).

That matters for PulseVM in two ways. The team already runs infrastructure inside the US payment system. And a PulseVM network does not have to choose between on-chain money and instant interbank payments: net positions between institutions can settle over FedNow.

## Working with credit unions today

- **Cornerstone League.** In October 2025 the league, which represents more than 600 credit unions across five states, partnered with Metallicus to give its members the **Metallicus Stablecoin Pilot**: a controlled sandbox with simulated reserves where a credit union can design, test and run its own branded stablecoin before production, at no cost as a member benefit ([source](https://metallicus.com/blog/cornerstone-credit-union-league-stablecoins)).
- **Metal Blockchain Banking Innovation Program.** Credit unions and associations work with Metallicus through workshops, research and development grants and custom builds on digital identity, private networks and tokenization. Participants include [Vibrant Credit Union](https://www.metallicus.com/blog/vibrant-credit-union-blockchain) (2024), [Directions Credit Union](https://metalblockchain.org/news-insights/directions-credit-union-blockchain) (2025) and the [MD|DC Credit Union Association](https://www.metallicus.com/blog/mddc).

::: info What this is, and what it is not
These are relationships with Metallicus and Metal Blockchain. They are not PulseVM production deployments. PulseVM itself is at test-network stage, and institutions start with a [pilot](/institutions/pilot).
:::

## Metal Dollar

Metal Dollar (XMD) is Metallicus' reserve-backed stablecoin index: a smart-contract basket of regulated dollar stablecoins, which can be minted or redeemed 1:1 against the stablecoins in the basket ([source](https://metallicus.com/metal-dollar)). It is separate from the tokenized deposits an institution issues on its own PulseVM network, which remain that institution's liability.
