PulseVM proton atom — hero mark preview

Pure SVG + CSS (no JS, no external assets, ~13 KB). Nucleus beats a slow lub-dub and emits hairline pulse rings; three electrons ride tilted elliptical orbits with real depth — they pass behind the nucleus and return in front, growing and brightening as they near the viewer. The whole orbital plane precesses slowly; the middle (warm) electron runs counter-rotation. Palette is keyed to the site accent gradient #4F7CFF → #8B95FF with a single warm accent.

  • Motion: transform/opacity only — cheap to composite. Orbit periods 12 s / 18 s / 26 s, heartbeat 3.4 s, precession 150 s.
  • Reduced motion: with prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the mark renders as a clean static atom (electrons parked at 35°/150°/250°, pulse rings hidden).
  • Theming: loaded via <img> it adapts to OS prefers-color-scheme; inlined into the page it follows the VitePress .dark toggle exactly (the stylesheet targets both). The art is deliberately legible on either background, so a mismatch is never jarring.
  • Tuning: everything is a CSS variable at the top of the SVG — brand hues, heartbeat period --B, and per-theme intensities (--halo, --ring, --arc, --tick, --field).
